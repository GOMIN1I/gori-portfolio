01 / Saidright

Saidright: dictation that teaches you the language

A free, open-source Mac app. Press the right ⌘, speak, and your words land where you were typing. If you’re learning English, it suggests one fix that matters.

My role
Product, interface, code, site, releases, ad campaign
Shipped
Five public releases in two days, 0.5 to 1.2
Runs on
Apple silicon, macOS 14 and later. Speech is recognised on the Mac.
Price
Free, Apache 2.0, no account

The campaign: “Voice takes shape”

One idea from the app’s own icon: voice bars become a coral cursor, the cursor becomes formatted text. I wrote the brief, directed it and signed off every cut. 3D in Blender, the edit in After Effects and Remotion, the score timed to the picture.

One idea, three formats

A film for the site, a short vertical cut for Reels and Shorts, and a poster. Each one made in English and in Russian.

Picture and sound, one hand

I cut the picture to the music and shaped the music around the picture, so every move lands where you feel it.

02 / the refund case

Refunding an order when the old payment portal is gone

From 15 September 2026, shops that moved from Klarna to Kustom lost access to the old portal. Refunds on old orders became their job. One mistake means paying twice, or paying back more than was charged.

  1. Map

    Rebuilt the process

    Reconstructed today’s flow from public docs and designed the target one in BPMN, with Support, Finance and Accounting lanes.

  2. Define

    Wrote the rules

    Ten business rules and ten requirements, each traced to the test that proves it.

  3. Prove

    Tested it in PostgreSQL

    A schema and a working register prototype. 42 of 42 SQL checks and 19 of 19 browser checks passed.
